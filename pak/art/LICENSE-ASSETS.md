Original authors: libretro team and contributors. The file is redistributed

ScummVM pak's flat icon.

`J2ME-photo.png` is an AI-generated render (Google Gemini) of a fictional,
unbranded candybar phone showing an original pixel-art game. It was supplied
for this pak on October 18, 2026; reference-image details were not supplied.
The white background was keyed to transparency, the transparent margins were
trimmed, and the phone was scaled with Pillow Lanczos filtering to fill the
height of a 384x384 canvas. The 2048x2048 JPEG original and the keyed
full-size master are kept in the private `umrk-assets` repository at
`Systems/ICONS/originals/J2ME.jpg` and `Systems/ICONS/J2ME.png`. Purely
AI-generated images carry no copyright of their own, and no rights are claimed
in the render itself. Google marks Gemini output with an invisible SynthID
watermark.

Phone and platform trade dress remains the property of the respective rights
holders and appears here to identify the platform.

## If you replace it

- Keep flat icons 256x256 RGBA and photographic icons square RGBA PNGs. The
  bundled photographic pack uses 384x384 runtime copies.
- Record where your replacement came from in this file, with its license.
- Public-domain photography, AI renders, and CC BY-SA material all carry
  different obligations, and they do not all survive redistribution on an SD
  card image. Check before you ship.
